# Delivery times

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery takes three to five working days to most pin codes.
Remote areas can take up to ten working days.

## Tracking

A tracking link is sent by email once the parcel leaves the warehouse.
Customers asking where their parcel is should be given that link.

## Delays

If a parcel has not arrived ten working days after dispatch, a person on the support
team investigates with the courier.
