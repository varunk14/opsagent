# Change of mind

## Eligibility

A customer who no longer wants an item may ask for their money back within 30 days
of delivery, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

## What is paid back

The full item price is paid back. Delivery fees are not refundable for change of mind.

## Exclusions

Personalised items, perishable goods and gift cards cannot be returned for change of mind.
