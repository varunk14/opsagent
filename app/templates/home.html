OpsAgent

A support agent that reads customer messages across email, Telegram, and voice notes, reasons through them with a local model, and only sends what a policy allows. Every run on this site is real. Nothing on this page is mocked.

email telegram voice form · off for the demo

How a run flows

1 · Intake
Read the message
Poll the channel, refuse anything too large, ignore bounces and auto-replies, record the message once (idempotent on the channel’s own id).
2 · Plan
Classify · extract · retrieve · plan
A local Llama-3.1 8B model classifies the intent, extracts the fields, retrieves the relevant policy passages, and proposes a next step. Every model call is validated against a schema and counted.
3 · Guardrail
Only what the ledger allows
A separate check reads the plan and asks: is the amount within policy, is this a duplicate refund, is confidence high enough? A no here hands the case to a person.
4 · Reply
Send only what was approved
The action commits inside a single transaction with the outbox row. A reply is sent once, marked once. Voice replies are synthesised and played back on the run’s page.
